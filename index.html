FinGuard AI — Multi-Platform Financial Threat Protection

FinGuard AI is a multi-platform financial scam detection system designed to identify suspicious financial activities, transactions, UPI requests, AutoPay mandates, and potentially fraudulent communication. The system combines rule-based analysis, behavioral analysis, machine learning concepts, and AI-powered risk analysis to detect suspicious patterns and provide understandable risk alerts.

01 — Problem & Solution

The Challenge

Financial scams occur across transactions, UPI collect requests, stealth AutoPay mandates, and digital communications (SMS, phishing messages, social channels). Traditional security only flags post-transaction fraud or relies on rigid blacklists without contextual or behavioral threat reasoning.

The FinGuard Solution

FinGuard AI introduces multi-stage risk evaluation analyzing transactional anomalies, mandate frequency, communication intent, and behavioral baseline deviations to compute an explainable risk score before financial loss occurs.

02 — Multi-Vector Detection Areas
Transactions

Velocity checks, sudden surge in amounts, high-frequency micro-debits, and anomalous time-of-day transfers.

UPI Requests

Auditing unverified collect requests, fake merchant handles, and reverse payment phishing traps.

AutoPay & Mandates

Detecting hidden recurring mandates, abnormal recurring caps, and deceptive subscription enrollments.

SMS & Messaging

Parsing urgent payment links, fake KYC update alerts, and deceptive bank impersonation messages.

Behavioral Anomalies

Detecting divergence from habitual receiver profiles, geolocation shifts, and uncharacteristic payment volume.

Real-Time Alerts

Clear, explainable threat notifications empowering users with immediate block or verification guidance.
